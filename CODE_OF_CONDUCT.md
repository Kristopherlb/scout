# Code of conduct

We want participation in LFD Evals to be safe, constructive, and welcoming.

Be respectful. Discuss ideas and behavior rather than attacking people. Make
room for different backgrounds and levels of experience. Do not harass,
threaten, demean, discriminate against, or expose private information about
another participant.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome; sustained hostility is not.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be
reviewed as confidentially and promptly as practical. Maintainers involved in
a report must recuse themselves from handling it.
